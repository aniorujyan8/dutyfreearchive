About

Browse our library of contemporary cigarette packets from across the globe. Follow user journeys as well as submitting your own insights. Delve into international smoking culture and participate in the live experience. This site is updated daily and retains submissions from 2026 onwards. Got a pack you love and don't see? Submit by email via the button below and our team will confirm and upload your recommendations accordingly. Interested in what we are doing? Start browsing the archive now!
